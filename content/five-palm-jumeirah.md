# FIVE Palm Jumeirah, Dubai

FIVE Palm Jumeirah is FIVE's flagship beachfront resort on the trunk of Dubai's
Palm Jumeirah, styled as the "Hottest Hotel in Dubai" and a
"dare-to-be-different immersive playground" — the brand invitation is simply
"Come Play". The resort combines a 150-metre private beach with a social pool
scene, award-winning restaurants and some of the city's best-known nightlife.

## Rooms and suites

Accommodation ranges from Superior rooms and Luxe Sea View rooms through
Junior Suites, One and Two Bedroom Suites, Skyline Suites and XL Terrace Pool
Suites, up to multi-bedroom Penthouses (two to five bedrooms), Townhouses,
Duplexes and Beach Villas. One- to three-bedroom Hotel Apartments are also
available for longer stays. Many suites and villas have private pools or
oversized terraces overlooking the Palm and the Dubai Marina skyline.

## Dining

The resort's restaurants include Cinque (Italian, recognised in the Michelin
Guide), Maiden Shanghai (contemporary Chinese, recognised by Gault & Millau),
The Penthouse (Japanese fusion dining with skyline views), Jade (Pan-Asian),
Praia (Californian beach-house dining), Numa (Arabic flavours with
international influences), Beach by FIVE (sushi, seafood and modern European
by the sand), Detox Me Café (health-focused menus with gluten-free, keto and
vegan options) and The Delisserie (homemade pastries and casual all-day fare).

## Nightlife and entertainment

Bohemia is the resort's beach-club concept with international DJs on the sand,
and The Penthouse doubles as a rooftop nightclub that DJ Mag has ranked the
number one club in the Middle East. BLING is the urban-music nightclub —
Afrobeat and hip-hop, with supercar arrivals encouraged — and The Social Pool
is "Dubai's hottest social pool" with live entertainment through the day.
Maiden Shanghai also offers private karaoke. Expect a packed weekly social
calendar across the pool, beach and rooftop.

## Wellness

ReFIVE Spa is the in-house spa, with treatments and reformer Pilates, joined
by Beverly Hills Wellness and Aesthetics, the Move at FIVE fitness programme
and a 24-hour gym.

## Recognition

FIVE Palm Jumeirah and its venues have been recognised by the Forbes Travel
Guide, the Michelin Guide (Cinque, 2024), Gault & Millau, BBC Good Food Middle
East (2024) and DJ Mag.
