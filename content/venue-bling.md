# BLING

BLING is the urban-music nightclub at FIVE Palm Jumeirah — the invitation is
literally to "drive into the hottest nightclub in your supercar". The
soundtrack is Dubai's best urban sounds: Afrobeat, hip-hop and urban club
tracks, a deliberate counterpoint to the house-led programming at The
Penthouse and Bohemia.

Table bookings and the current event calendar are on blingdxb.com; door
policy and dress code apply as at all FIVE club venues.
