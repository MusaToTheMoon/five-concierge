# Lío Ibiza

Lío is the Pacha Group's cabaret-restaurant-club at Marina Ibiza — "the
original and most outrageous Lío in the world", open since 2011 and an Isla
Blanca institution. The room opens to the water with views of Dalt Vila, and
the stage appears to float above a raised pool.

## The experience

Dinner and show first: a Mediterranean menu of lobster, red prawns, caviar,
Angus and Wagyu beef (gluten-free and vegan options available), staged
around a full cabaret production — the 2026 season's show is "Halftime
Show", "an explosive cocktail of performance, pop and pure mischief". After
the show the venue becomes Lío Club, with international DJs nightly; the
house motto is "what happens at Lío stays at Lío".

## Dress code

Elegant attire required — no beachwear, swimsuits, shorts, sandals, bare
torsos or vests.

## Recognition

Best Concept Restaurant Award, Ciutat d'Eivissa 2026, presented by Ibiza
Town Hall.
