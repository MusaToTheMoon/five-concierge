# Playa Pacha Dubai

Playa Pacha brings "Ibiza's legendary entertainment brand" to the JBR
beachfront at FIVE LUXE, billed as Dubai's largest pool and beach club
experience, on the ground floor at The Walk, Jumeirah Beach Residence. The
formula is Mediterranean cuisine, house-music culture and multiple pool
zones running day to night, capped by the PACHA ICONS series, "the world's
most exclusive music experience", with headline house artists.

## Weekly events

- Wet Cherry Ladies Day: Wednesdays, 12 PM to 5 PM, complimentary access
  for ladies.
- Balearic Beats: Thursdays from 12 PM.
- Sun/Sets: Fridays from 12 PM.
- La Musica: Saturdays from 12 PM.

## Hours and access

Open daily 10 AM to 8 PM. Weekday entry is complimentary with premium
loungers and VIP beds bookable; weekend access and bed pricing vary by day
and season; check the venue site for current rates.
