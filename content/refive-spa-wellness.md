# ReFIVE Spa and wellness at FIVE

ReFIVE Spa is FIVE's in-house spa brand, present at FIVE Palm Jumeirah, FIVE
LUXE JBR, FIVE Jumeirah Village and FIVE Zurich. The positioning is recovery
for people who play hard: treatments, rituals and studio classes designed to
reset between beach days and club nights.

## The flagship: ReFIVE at FIVE Palm Jumeirah

Open Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 10 PM and Friday to Sunday 9 AM to midnight.
Signature experiences include:

- An award-winning hammam, named Top Hammam Experience in the Middle East
  by the World Luxury Spa Awards, with Kessa scrub, Ghassoul clay mask and
  assisted stretching.
- Sensorial Head Spa rituals (solo or couples), combining scalp
  consultation, lymphatic-drainage facial work and a glow mask.
- Facials using SODASHI organic skincare and Natura Bissé actives.
- Sports recovery massage, steam room, ice-bath therapy, and a rooftop spa
  pool with hydrotherapy jets.
- Reformer Pilates in intimate classes capped at four people, plus bundled
  wellness packages combining Pilates, massage and pool access.
- A Brow Studio for lash extensions, lash lifts and brow sculpting.

Treatment pricing is published on the spa's booking page and changes with
the seasonal menu; book directly through the property.

## Across the other properties

FIVE Palm Jumeirah also hosts Beverly Hills Wellness and Aesthetics and the
Move at FIVE fitness programme with a 24-hour gym. At FIVE LUXE JBR and FIVE
Zurich, ReFIVE Spa is joined by The Gym, with The Salon at FIVE LUXE for
hair and beauty. FIVE Jumeirah Village adds The Salon and a Peaky Blinders
Barbershop for men's grooming. FIVE Zurich rounds out wellness with its
outdoor Social Pool (Zurich's only five-star outdoor pool experience), and
Destino FIVE Ibiza keeps guests moving at THE GYM.
