# ReFIVE Spa and wellness at FIVE

ReFIVE Spa is FIVE's in-house spa brand, present at FIVE Palm Jumeirah, FIVE
LUXE JBR, FIVE Jumeirah Village and FIVE Zurich. The positioning is recovery
for people who play hard: treatments, rituals and studio classes designed to
reset between beach days and club nights.

## What's on offer

At FIVE Palm Jumeirah, ReFIVE Spa offers treatments alongside reformer
Pilates, and the property also hosts Beverly Hills Wellness and Aesthetics
for medical-aesthetic services. Fitness runs through the Move at FIVE
programme and a 24-hour gym. At FIVE LUXE JBR and FIVE Zurich, ReFIVE Spa is
joined by The Gym, with The Salon at FIVE LUXE for hair and beauty. FIVE
Jumeirah Village adds The Salon and a Peaky Blinders Barbershop for men's
grooming. FIVE Zurich rounds out wellness with its outdoor Social Pool, and
Destino FIVE Ibiza keeps guests moving at THE GYM.

## Booking

Spa treatments, salon appointments and fitness classes are bookable directly
with each property. Treatment menus and pricing are not published in this
demo's sources — contact the spa through the property's page on
fivehotelsandresorts.com for the current menu.
