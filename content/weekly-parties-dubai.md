# The FIVE Dubai week, night by night

A guide to the recurring weekly programming across FIVE's three Dubai
properties. Line-ups rotate seasonally — always confirm dates and tickets on
each venue's page.

## Monday to Thursday

Big Mood Monday and Tuesday's ladies night (Club Hits & Premium Sips) run at
The Penthouse, FIVE Palm Jumeirah. Wednesdays bring ToyRoom to The
Penthouse, Wet Cherry Ladies Day to Playa Pacha at FIVE LUXE (12 PM to 5 PM,
complimentary for ladies) and Unwine at Maiden Shanghai from 8 PM. Thursdays
offer Groove Studio at The Penthouse, Balearic Beats at Playa Pacha and
Queen of Hearts at Paradiso.

## Friday

Skyline Night Brunch at The Penthouse from 8 PM, Sun/Sets at Playa Pacha
from noon, LOST at Bohemia in the afternoon (12:30 PM to 4:30 PM), Moonlight
Splash night swimming at Bohemia from 7 PM, House Beats at Paradiso from
9 PM, and Urban Beats at FIVE Jumeirah Village.

## Saturday

La Musica at Playa Pacha from noon, the SoulCaliCool Pool Party Brunch and
ToyRoom Cabaret at FIVE Jumeirah Village, Moonlight Splash again at Bohemia,
After Brunch at Paradiso from 4 PM and House Beats from 9 PM. Maiden
Shanghai's Naughty Noodles brunch and afterparty also run on weekends.

## Sunday

Bohemia Sunday Brunch (1 PM to 5 PM) flowing into Sundowner from 5:30 PM,
and Sundays at The Penthouse from 10 PM. Mimi's Pool Club at FIVE Jumeirah
Village adds Late Night Swim and SOS events through the season.
