# Frequently asked questions

## Where are FIVE's hotels?

FIVE operates six properties across three destinations: FIVE Palm Jumeirah,
FIVE LUXE JBR and FIVE Jumeirah Village in Dubai; FIVE Zurich in Switzerland;
and Destino FIVE Ibiza and Pacha Hotel Ibiza on Ibiza, Spain.

## How do I book a room, table or spa treatment?

Book directly through the official site at fivehotelsandresorts.com — each
property page handles room reservations, restaurant bookings and spa
appointments. This concierge cannot check availability, quote rates or make
bookings; prices and availability change constantly and are only accurate on
the official booking channels.

## What is the connection between FIVE and Pacha?

FIVE's parent company, FIVE Holdings, acquired The Pacha Group — including
the iconic Pacha Ibiza nightclub, Destino and El Hotel Pacha. That is why
Pacha concepts like Playa Pacha, Pure Pacha and Pacha ICONS appear at FIVE
LUXE JBR in Dubai and across the Ibiza resorts, and why guests at the Ibiza
hotels get privileged access to the Pacha Ibiza club.

## Do hotel guests get access to the nightlife venues?

Yes, with property-specific perks: FIVE LUXE guests get complimentary Pure
Pacha access, FIVE Jumeirah Village guests get complimentary entry to
sister-property events (Bohemia Presents at the Palm; Pure Pacha and Pacha
ICONS at FIVE LUXE), Destino FIVE Ibiza guests get free Pacha Ibiza
nightclub entry, and Pacha Hotel Ibiza guests use a dedicated VIP entrance
to the club. Club nights remain subject to door policy and age restrictions.

## What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in and check-out times vary by property and rate. Confirm them on your
booking confirmation or the property's page at fivehotelsandresorts.com.

## Are the hotels family-friendly?

The resorts host all guests, but FIVE's identity leans adult: pool parties,
DJ programming and nightlife are central to the experience. Families should
review each property's page to pick the right fit — quieter room categories
and beach areas exist, especially at the larger resorts.

## How do I contact FIVE?

Through the contact details and enquiry forms on fivehotelsandresorts.com and
each property's own site. For nightlife tables and events, use the venue
pages; for Pacha Ibiza tickets, see the official Pacha channels.
