# The Penthouse Dubai

The Penthouse crowns FIVE Palm Jumeirah as "Dubai's #1 rooftop club and
lounge" — ranked the number one club in the Middle East by DJ Mag — pairing
contemporary Japanese dining (live grill, sushi, elevated mixology) with
skyline views and international DJs. It won BBC Good Food Middle East
recognition in 2024.

## The weekly rhythm

- Monday — Big Mood Monday
- Tuesday — Club Hits & Premium Sips ladies night
- Wednesday — ToyRoom
- Thursday — Groove Studio
- Friday — Skyline Night Brunch from 8 PM
- Sunday — Sundays at The Penthouse from 10 PM

Events typically start between 8 and 10 PM, with lounge tables and club VIP
zones bookable through the venue.
