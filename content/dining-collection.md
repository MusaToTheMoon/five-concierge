# Dining across FIVE Hotels and Resorts

FIVE's culinary programme spans award-winning fine dining, beachfront grills
and late-night street food across Dubai, Zurich and Ibiza. Highlights by
craving:

## Italian

Cinque at FIVE Palm Jumeirah is the group's Michelin Guide-recognised Italian
flagship. Trattoria by Cinque at FIVE Jumeirah Village serves a more casual
take, and Cinque Studio at FIVE Zurich gives the concept a Swiss twist.

## Asian

Maiden Shanghai at FIVE Palm Jumeirah is the celebrated contemporary Chinese
restaurant, recognised by Gault & Millau, with private karaoke rooms. Jade
covers Pan-Asian flavours at the Palm, The Penthouse serves Japanese fusion
above the Palm Jumeirah skyline, and Ronin at FIVE LUXE JBR is a gourmet
Japanese concept with live cooking.

## Mediterranean and beach dining

Praia at FIVE Palm Jumeirah does Californian beach-house dining; Beach by FIVE
plates sushi, seafood and modern European by the sand. Cielo, at both FIVE
LUXE JBR and Destino FIVE Ibiza, is the Mediterranean all-day concept, and
Elia at Destino FIVE Ibiza serves cliffside Greek cuisine at sunset. Numa at
the Palm brings Arabic flavours with international influences.

## Street food and casual

Soul St., at FIVE Jumeirah Village and FIVE Zurich, is FIVE's gourmet
street-food and cocktail concept. Goose Island Tap House (FIVE Jumeirah
Village and FIVE LUXE) pairs craft beer with live sports. One for the Road at
FIVE Jumeirah Village pours artisan coffee 24 hours a day, The Lobby Lounge
at FIVE LUXE serves artisan coffee and fresh pastries, The Delisserie at
the Palm handles pastries and casual bites, and Detox Me Café covers
health-focused, gluten-free, keto and vegan menus.

## Bars

Paradiso at FIVE LUXE is the cocktail bar celebrated on the World's Best Bars
circuit; Tête-à-Tête at FIVE LUXE is a French Monte Carlo-style bistro-bar;
The Vault at FIVE Zurich is the wine bar and cigar lounge.

FIVE does not publish menu prices on these pages. For menus, hours and
reservations, book through each restaurant's page on the FIVE website.
