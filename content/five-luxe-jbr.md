# FIVE LUXE, JBR Dubai

FIVE LUXE is FIVE's beachfront resort at Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) in
Dubai, positioned as "Dubai's Most Glam Beach Resort" with the promise of
"Ibiza vibes, Dubai style". It is the home of FIVE's Pacha partnership in
Dubai, bringing the legendary Ibiza brand to the JBR beachfront.

## Rooms and suites

Room types run from Superior and Superior Studio Sea View rooms, Luxe Sea View
rooms and Luxe Spa Lofts through Junior Suites (including sea-view options) to
an extensive suite collection: Skyline Suites with private pools (one to four
bedrooms), Poolside Rooms and Suites with direct pool access, Pool Terrace
Suites, Luxe Spa Suites, three-bedroom Sensoria Suites with pools, Euphoria
Suites, four-bedroom Sensoria Residences, and the top-tier Encore Suite and
Masterpiece Suite with private pool.

## Dining and bars

Signature venues include Ronin (gourmet Japanese with live cooking), Paradiso
(a cocktail bar concept celebrated on the World's Best Bars circuit),
Tête-à-Tête (a French Monte Carlo-style bistro and bar), Cielo (all-day dining
with a Mediterranean focus), Goose Island Tap House (a sports pub with gourmet
comfort food) and Playa Pacha, the all-day Ibiza-style pool and party venue.

## Beach club and nightlife

Playa Pacha is the resort's beach and pool party destination, and FIVE LUXE
hosts Pacha ICONS and Pure Pacha event programming; hotel guests enjoy
complimentary access to Pure Pacha events. The vibe is a Mediterranean
open-air party transplanted to the Arabian Gulf.

## Wellness

ReFIVE Spa, The Gym and The Salon cover spa treatments, fitness and grooming.

## Recognition

FIVE LUXE has been recognised by the Forbes Travel Guide and the Michelin
Guide, with awards attention from CN Traveller Middle East Readers' Choice
Awards and the What's On Dubai Awards.
