# Sustainable Indulgence

FIVE brands its ESG programme "Sustainable Indulgence" — the claim that a
"vibrant luxury" lifestyle can run inside a "Global Sustainable Entertainment
Ecosystem". Sustainability is managed at group level by FIVE Holdings, with a
published ESG agenda, a net-zero build-tech design concept for new
developments, and investor-grade compliance reporting.

## Verified property-level claims

Destino FIVE Ibiza holds LEED Platinum v4 BD+C certification with 80 points,
sources 100% green electricity as of 2024, and reduced water consumption per
guest by 10% in 2024 compared with 2023.

FIVE Zurich won at the Sustainable Luxury Awards 2024 and markets itself as a
sustainable design-and-lifestyle destination.

## What this means for guests

The brand's position is that the parties, pools and fine dining run on
cleaner inputs — green energy sourcing, water-use reduction and certified
building standards — without asking guests to compromise on the experience.
