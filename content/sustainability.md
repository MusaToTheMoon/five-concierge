# Sustainable Indulgence

FIVE brands its ESG programme "Sustainable Indulgence": the claim that a
"vibrant luxury" lifestyle can run inside a "Global Sustainable Entertainment
Ecosystem". Sustainability is managed at group level by FIVE Holdings, with a
published ESG agenda, a net-zero build-tech design concept for new
developments, and investor-grade compliance reporting.

## Certifications

FIVE Palm Jumeirah and FIVE Jumeirah Village each hold LEED Platinum
certification plus a 3-Star SPIRE Smart Building rating. FIVE Zurich is LEED
Platinum certified, the only hotel in Switzerland with the certification at
the time of award, scoring 84 points, the second-highest LEED score
globally. Destino FIVE Ibiza holds LEED Platinum v4 BD+C certification with
80 points. The group also carries ISO certifications spanning quality
(9001), environment (14001), health and safety (45001), energy (50001),
food safety (22000) and information security (27001).

## Energy and carbon

FIVE achieved 100% renewable electricity in 2022 via International Renewable
Energy Certificates, and its Dubai hotels were the first in the UAE to source
100% renewable energy through DEWA's Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum Solar
Park. Carbon use intensity is down 76.2% against the 2020 baseline. Destino
FIVE Ibiza sources 100% green electricity (2024).

## Water and waste

FIVE Jumeirah Village runs a greywater system servicing its 150+ pools, and
FIVE Palm Jumeirah heats water with solar power and repurposes it for
landscaping. Destino FIVE Ibiza cut water use per guest by 10% in 2024
versus 2023. Biodegradable amenities run across properties, with a glass
bottling plant and composting system coming to FIVE Jumeirah Village.

## What this means for guests

The parties, pools and fine dining run on cleaner inputs (certified
buildings, renewable energy and measured water reduction) without asking
guests to compromise on the experience. Current certifications and ESG
reports live on five-holdings.com.
