# Pacha Hotel Ibiza

Pacha Hotel Ibiza (El Hotel Pacha) is the boutique hotel of the Pacha family,
located in one of Ibiza's most exclusive quarters, directly opposite the
legendary Pacha nightclub and steps from Marina Ibiza. It joined the FIVE
family with the acquisition of The Pacha Group, under the shared promise of
"Elevating Luxury, Redefining Entertainment".

## Rooms

Three room categories keep it intimate: Superior rooms (25 square metres with
a private terrace), Junior Suites (33 square metres with terrace) and the
Master Suite (41 square metres with a sprawling 128-square-metre terrace).
All rooms have king beds.

## Dining and events

Double Cherry is the hotel's house-music dining experience with weekly
Wednesday events from 8pm, and Native hosts Saturday-evening dining with live
DJs. The Pacha Hotel Brunch runs daily from 11:30am to 5pm, and Football
Nights screen match days with food and drink offers.

## Nightlife perk

The defining privilege: hotel guests enter the Pacha Ibiza nightclub across
the street through a dedicated VIP entrance. Pacha has been the island's most
iconic club since 1973, and staying at the hotel puts its dancefloor on your
doorstep.

## Press

The hotel has been featured by the Telegraph, Grazia, Rolling Stone and DJ
Mag as a luxury base for Ibiza's nightlife.
