# Paradiso

Paradiso at FIVE LUXE JBR is the Dubai home of the bar concept celebrated at
the top of the World's Best Bars circuit — a space of "magical surrealism"
where, as the bar puts it, "you do not need to imagine, the unimaginable is
real". The theatrical room layers flowers, crystals and elephant sculptures
across multiple zones, with a rooftop terrace for private events.

## Signature cocktails

Evolution Negroni, Kriptonite, Mango Chachachá, Tesla and The Illusionist —
sensory-driven serves built around the surrealist theme.

## Weekly events

- Queen of Hearts — Thursdays from 7 PM.
- House Beats — Fridays and Saturdays from 9 PM.
- After Brunch — Saturdays from 4 PM.

## Recognition

Featured in Time Out Dubai's best-restaurants list (2026), shortlisted at the
Time Out Dubai Restaurant Awards (2025) and featured at the FACT Dining
Awards Dubai (2024).
