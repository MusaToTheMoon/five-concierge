# Pacha Ibiza

Pacha Ibiza calls itself "the world's most iconic nightclub" — the
cherry-logo institution that has anchored the island's nightlife since 1973
and is now part of the FIVE family through FIVE Holdings' acquisition of The
Pacha Group. Club nights run 11 PM to 6 AM in season.

## Resident nights (2026 season)

- Wednesdays — BLOND:ISH presents ABRACADABRA
- Thursdays — Purple Disco Machine with Pure Pacha
- Fridays — Marco Carola presents Music On
- Saturdays — Roger Sanchez with Flower Power
- Sundays — Solomun+1

## Guest perks

Guests of Destino FIVE Ibiza receive free entry to the club, and Pacha Hotel
Ibiza guests cross the street through a dedicated VIP entrance. VIP zone
bookings include complimentary drinks equal to the booking value — current
options and pricing are on pacha.com.

## The wider Pacha family

Restaurante Pacha, the Pacha ICONS open-air series (hosted at Destino FIVE
Ibiza and Playa Pacha Dubai), Destino FIVE Ibiza, Pacha Hotel Ibiza and the
Pacha Collection merchandise line.
